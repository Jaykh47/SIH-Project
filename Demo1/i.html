Land Stack DPI Architectural Blueprint

Problem Statement Fulfillment:

This prototype demonstrates how fragmented land datasets (Revenue, Town Planning, Registration, Utilities) are federated around a single spatial key: ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar).

1. Base Layer Georeferenced cadastral boundaries, ground-truthed polygons, and unique 14-digit ULPIN keys.
2. Essential Layers Record of Rights (RoR), Sub-Registrar deed chain, Master Plan zoning, and CERSAI mortgage registry.
3. Value-Add Layers Circle valuation rates, property taxation dues, municipal utility connections, and AI satellite change alarms.

How to interact with this demo:

  • Click on any parcel on the map to slide out the complete 360° Land Card ("Bhu-Kundali").
  • Switch thematic views on the left floating menu (e.g. Master Plan Zone, Dispute/Lien status).
  • Toggle AI Satellite Encroachment to witness how automated drone/satellite change detection flags illegal construction.
  • Switch to "Dept. Dashboard" at the top right to simulate cross-departmental approval workflows.
